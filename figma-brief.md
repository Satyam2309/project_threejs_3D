# Figma Design Brief — Wedding Event Manager

## Frame Sizes
- Desktop: **1440 x 1024**
- Tablet: **768 x 1024**
- Mobile: **375 x 812**

## Brand Direction
Elegant, warm, and romantic with modern readability. Design for guests first, with practical planning surfaces for couple/planner users.

## Color Palette
- Primary: `#6F4E37` (espresso)
- Secondary: `#D9B8A0` (rose beige)
- Accent: `#B76E79` (muted rose)
- Surface Light: `#FFF9F4`
- Surface Dark: `#232323`
- Text Light: `#1F1F1F`
- Text Dark: `#F1F1F1`

## Typography (Google Fonts)
- Display/Headings: **Playfair Display** (500, 700)
- Body/UI: **Inter** (400, 500, 600, 700)

## Components to Include
- Top nav with role + theme controls
- Hero section with countdown and CTA
- Timeline cards (Our Story)
- Venue details card + map placeholder
- RSVP form states (default, validation error, success modal)
- Registry item cards and group gift tags
- Schedule accordion day cards
- Planner widgets: guest table, CSV action, seating drag-and-drop, budget progress, tasks
- Vendor cards and profile modal
- FAQ accordion and contact form
